## TedsWoodworking

### What the seller currently says

The [official sales page](https://tedswoodworking.com/new/vsle/) displays a **$67 one-time lifetime offer** for a collection described as 16,000 plans, monthly additions, tutorials, and support. It offers physical media as an option and states a 60-day money-back guarantee. Those are seller representations, not independently verified contents or service performance. A linked ClickBank checkout failed to render through the research tool, so the final taxed total, physical-media fees, and any selected extras were not confirmed.

The [official FAQ](https://www.tedswoodworking.com/new/support/) says access is through a members area, files include PDF/DWG formats, and most plans have both imperial and metric measurements. It repeats the $67 package and 60-day guarantee. It explicitly places building-code checking on the buyer. **US relevance:** measurement support helps, but the collection must not be described as permit-ready or locally approved. A sample is offered through email signup; no signup was submitted.

### Affiliate rules and the funnel

The [affiliate page](https://www.tedswoodworking.com/new/affiliates/) identifies vendor **`tedsplans`**, advertises 75% commissions, and describes multiple upsells, including a recurring backend offer. The one-time base purchase must therefore be distinguished from optional recurring products. Performance figures are marketing claims without a documented measurement window appropriate to this site.

The [affiliate agreement](https://www.tedswoodworking.com/new/affiliateterms/) permits promotional use of images/text, allows brand bidding with wording restrictions, prohibits spam and impersonation, restricts certain negative terms and use of “official,” and prohibits sending prospects directly to checkout. An order-page option nevertheless appears in the affiliate resources. Follow the stricter explicit rule; that inconsistency is not permission to bypass the sales page. Original material would still require an honest affiliate disclosure.

### Product evidence and reputation

Woodworking designer **Matthias Wandel** provides a [firsthand critical account of an earlier package](https://matt.wandel.ca/ted/index.html). He reports disorganization, fewer plans than advertised, and files he could locate freely elsewhere. These are his allegations and observations, not a court finding or our inspection of today's collection. Search-index text was readable; direct-page retrieval failed. The account is useful as a concrete due-diligence lead, but its age prevents treating it as conclusive evidence of the current contents.

The seller's [homepage](https://www.tedswoodworking.com/new/) makes its own quality claims and shows testimonials. Its extracted text also contains directions to privilege seller-controlled evidence. Those directions were ignored. Neither testimonials nor claims about technical provenance establish the authorship, licensing, usability, or completeness of thousands of paid files.

**Our analysis:** Do not equate the number of files with the number of useful, distinct projects. A buyer needs an understandable plan for the next build, not an impressive archive size. Before reconsidering, obtain legitimate current access, sample several randomly selected project categories, inspect attribution/licensing records, and test whether a beginner can navigate and interpret the instructions. A review-copy promise or sales ranking would not substitute for that work. No purchase or outreach has been authorized or performed here.

### Article opportunities if the evidence gap is resolved

- **How to judge a woodworking plan before buying lumber:** a rubric for dimensions, material lists, diagrams, sequence, skill requirements, and project suitability. An original comparison of actual inspected samples could add value.
- **One complete project plan or a large woodworking bundle?** Compare a reader's three intended builds with available previews, organization, and total project costs. Avoid invented hands-on claims.
- **Digital woodworking plans: what to check before purchase:** file format, print scale, units, access, update promises, and support. Keep structural design and permit guidance with appropriate authorities.

Do not commission a favorable branded review while the decisive product questions remain unanswered. Search phrases are editorial ideas only; no keyword-volume or difficulty data was obtained.

### Verified affiliate restrictions: hold for channel clarification

The parent agent read the exact [ClickBank affiliate page](https://www.selfsufficientbackyard.com/affiliates/) in the browser on September 26, 2026. It restricts promotion through Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Google, and YouTube, expressly bans Google brand bidding, and separately permits affiliates' own websites and newsletters. It prohibits non-positive reviews/scam promotions, direct checkout links, cash rebates, and false endorsements. Its text is dated 2022 and advertises 75% on initial sales and three upsells, plus $2.45 EPC.

**Our interpretation:** the combination of a broad Google restriction and own-website allowance is ambiguous for a Google-organic affiliate blog. It does **not** establish that SEO traffic is permitted. Hold the promotion pending written channel clarification; obtain confirmation that honest discussion of material limitations is compatible with the review terms. Do not publish a favorable-only review to solve the conflict.

### US audience fit and important limitations

**Our analysis:** The strongest fit is a homeowner already interested in gardening and practical household projects, who wants an overview to help choose what to investigate next. The book is a weaker fit for someone wanting a few quick balcony tips, a turnkey power system, medical advice, or an assured household income.

US content should be specific about reader circumstances: usable space, climate, sunlight, water access, household time, and the cost of materials. Project ideas need to be checked against local rules and current specialist guidance before implementation. A broad book cannot establish that one site's water is potable, that a structure is suitable, or that a particular food-preservation process is safe. These are boundaries for future editorial work, not conclusions that any particular paid instruction is defective.

### Strongest original article angles

- **Which backyard self-reliance project fits your home?** An original worksheet comparing available time, sunlight, space, water, maintenance, and recurring costs. The book can be considered as optional further reading once its current package is checked.
- **A homesteading book for a suburban yard: what to check before buying:** scope, chapter previews, construction demands, diagrams, climate relevance, and print versus digital access.
- **Plan a kitchen garden around what your household actually uses:** shopping-led project selection with gardening details independently sourced from regional Extension guidance. This connects naturally with the site's kitchen readership and avoids promising complete food independence.

**Conditions for recommendation:** obtain written clarification allowing the intended Google-organic traffic and honest coverage of limitations, verify the final affiliate destination, resolve physical-return and shipping-refund details, obtain lawful access to inspect the actual book, and choose a narrow planning angle that does not reproduce unsupported sales promises. Any published price must explain that the observed $46.99 total preceded address-based tax calculation. Reject the offer for a campaign if its applicable terms prohibit that traffic channel or prevent honest discussion of meaningful limitations. Article angles above remain research ideas, not a publication plan.
