# ClickBank expansion research: VINCheckup and Rocket Languages

Research date: September 26, 2026. Internal editorial assessment for a US audience. No purchases, report runs, lesson testing, applications, messages, account changes or publication were performed. Prices are public-page observations, not verified checkout totals. All recommendations below are editorial judgments, not revenue forecasts.

## Decision

| Candidate | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VINCheckup | Conditional; hold promotion pending clarification | Its NMVTIS supply/approval relationship, refund timing and account-specific commission need resolving. Automotive coverage is also a substantial expansion beyond home/kitchen buying guides. |
| Rocket Languages | Hold: currently unavailable to unproven affiliates | More clearly documented consumer offering, but the marketplace-linked route requires an established education-sales record. Consider only if eligibility is demonstrated, admission confirmed and an appropriate content section developed. |

Neither should displace relevant home/kitchen content merely because marketplace conversion figures look attractive. An organic visitor seeking a knife or cookbook is not automatically a qualified buyer for either service.

Root also observed the inspection/data limitations disclaimer. Web retrieval failed for this agent.
- [Exact ClickBank affiliate terms](https://www.vincheckup.com/affiliateNew/terms.php): root inspected these in the browser; they show an August 12, 2024 update date. They state 65% commission, with payments through the official affiliate network and an account required. This differs from the live marketplace description's 75%; confirm the user's actual rate. Standard text/banner links are supported after network acceptance. Affiliates must not imitate the site or appear to be its official operator. Preliminary/teaser search funnels outside VINCheckup's control require prior approval and manager-arranged API integration; do not embed an unapproved VIN-search widget. Reports remain subject to accuracy/completeness limitations and do not replace inspection. The terms were read only; nothing was accepted. Web retrieval remained unavailable to this agent.
- [Public affiliate center](https://www.vincheckup.com/affiliate/) advertises 65% commission, registration before promotion, link generation and marketing assets. It sends registration to [Moresby Media](https://affiliates.moresbymedia.com/affiliates/?pn=6076119fadc0a). This is a separate observed entry route; do not assume its advertised rate or contract equals the ClickBank account offer. The application's public terms area did not expose a substantive agreement in retrieved text.

The [ClickBank route's terms URL](https://www.vincheckup.com/cb/terms) returned navigation and a ClickBank retailer notice, without substantive purchase terms in extracted text. The accessible general terms are documented below. Do not substitute a generic ClickBank refund period for a verified product-specific checkout promise.

### Pricing, access and US scope

The [public pricing page](https://www.vincheckup.com/pricing) shows $19.95 for one report, $29.95 for three and $49.95 for ten. It says there is no recurring monthly billing and report downloads remain available for 30 days from purchase, with active membership required. It claims data across all 50 states; this does not establish complete coverage of every vehicle/event. The three-report card's $9.72 unit figure conflicts with its $29.95 total: division gives approximately $9.98. Use verified package totals, not that inconsistent unit claim.

The [ClickBank sales route](https://www.vincheckup.com/cb/) describes cars, trucks, vans, RVs and motorcycles and requires a 17-character VIN for vehicles after 1980. The main site/general terms use different wording around the 1981 boundary. Verify eligibility for an older vehicle instead of promising universal coverage. No non-US coverage was established.

### Report sample and data limitations

The [public sample report](https://www.vincheckup.com/sample-report) displays title, accident, salvage/total-loss, recall, lien, sale, odometer and other checks. It is an illustration, not evidence that any customer's report contains all those fields or that the service finds all incidents. No comparison of purchased reports or known vehicle histories was conducted.

The [general terms](https://www.vincheckup.com/terms), dated May 16, 2022 on the page, say data can be incomplete/inaccurate and reports do not replace inspection. Preliminary free results may differ from paid results. Unlimited VIN decoding is distinguished from paid history reports; charges can apply even without results. The terms claim an AAMVA-tested/approved NMVTIS connection and identify Vin, Inc. as an authorized provider. That is the seller's statement, not independent confirmation of VINCheckup's current provider status.

### Does the official NMVTIS list support approval claims?

I did not find VINCheckup or vincheckup.com among the providers or additional affiliated URLs displayed on the [US Department of Justice's current approved-provider list](https://vehiclehistory.bja.ojp.gov/nmvtis_vehiclehistory). **This does not prove illegitimacy or establish that it cannot obtain data through a reseller relationship.** It does mean this research does not support calling VINCheckup an approved NMVTIS provider. Obtain the named upstream provider and evidence covering this exact domain before making that assertion.

The [DOJ's explanation of an NMVTIS report](https://vehiclehistory.bja.ojp.gov/nmvtis_understandingvhr) focuses on title, brands, odometer, total-loss and salvage indicators; other services may add repair/recall data. It recommends independent inspection and other information alongside a report. A future guide should separate the federal database's scope from a vendor's supplementary data.

### Refund ambiguity

The [general terms](https://www.vincheckup.com/terms) advertise a first-time-customer 30-day satisfaction guarantee, describe refunds as case-by-case, then impose a four-week cutoff. Those durations differ. Do not write “unconditional 30-day refund” or “risk free.” Confirm the exact ClickBank checkout policy and how the seller reconciles the timing before promotion.

### Article opportunity and competition

Live searches used: `VINCheckup review`, `VINCheckup official affiliates pricing refund NMVTIS approved`, plus official-provider searches. Results included [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.vincheckup.com), a [detailed critical review](https://subhadeepghosh.com/vincheckup-review), [an exact-match affiliate review site](https://vincheckupreview.com/) and other reviews. These are observations of competing content, not adopted evidence about report accuracy. Their prices, accusations, ratings and claimed tests were not used to validate the product.

My assessment: branded intent is contested and trust-sensitive. Another seller-summary review is unlikely to offer a strong editorial distinction. Search volume, keyword difficulty, rank positions and expected traffic were not measured.

Potential original articles, only if ShoppeCove intentionally adds an automotive category:

- “VIN decoder vs vehicle history report: what are you paying for?” Include a field-by-field evidence checklist and free official alternatives after verifying them.
- “One car or a shortlist: choosing a vehicle-report package.” Compare total spend and expiration, without claiming one data provider is most accurate.
- “How to read a vehicle history report before a used-car inspection.” Requires informed automotive review and verified source interpretation.

Reconsider promotion after confirming the account's commission, upstream NMVTIS relationship, checkout/refunds and at least one permissioned, documented report comparison. The generator and affiliate terms were inspected by root, but those checks do not resolve the remaining issues. A general educational guide can still explain the buying problem without recommending this seller.

## Rocket Languages

### Affiliate admission and current restrictions

- [Marketplace-linked registration URL](https://affiliate.rocketlanguages.com/register) redirected to the partner-program destination but returned a notice saying new affiliates are not accepted unless they have demonstrated success selling education technology products/services. It directs eligible applicants through a ClickBank or CJ account manager. Root subsequently verified this same notice in the live browser; treat this as the actionable admission status.
- [Direct public partner page](https://www.rocketlanguages.com/partner-program) separately returned an open application presentation. It advertises 40% for content partners, 20% for coupon/rebate partners and 60% for top-performing partners, plus 30-day course access and brand guidance.
- [Public application form](https://www.rocketlanguages.com/partner-program/register) accepts ClickBank or Commission Junction identifiers and describes review/approval. These route differences are unresolved; an accessible form does not establish eligibility or approval.
- [Full partner terms](https://www.rocketlanguages.com/partner-program/terms) require compliance with ClickBank's contract, prohibit trademark PPC bidding (including competitor terms), trademarked domains, impersonation and verbatim copying. Promotional comparisons must be truthful; derogatory promotional wording and defamatory competitor claims are prohibited. Organic review content still needs to follow those conditions.

Use only the commission and permissions granted to the user's approved account. Do not present the maximum tier as the normal rate or claim ShoppeCove is accepted. No application was submitted.

### Course offering, prices and access

The [official home page](https://www.rocketlanguages.com/) markets 14 language options, audio-based lessons, language/culture instruction, pronunciation recognition, conversation practice and downloadable audio. These are advertised features, not independently tested learning outcomes. Public free trials are offered; no account was created.

Representative public list prices checked:

| Spanish Level 1 | $149.95 | [Spanish pricing](https://www.rocketlanguages.com/pricing?language=spanish) |
| Spanish Levels 1–2 | $299.90 | [Spanish pricing](https://www.rocketlanguages.com/pricing?language=spanish) |
| Spanish Levels 1–3 | $449.85 | [Spanish pricing](https://www.rocketlanguages.com/pricing?language=spanish) |
| English | $99.95 | [English pricing](https://www.rocketlanguages.com/pricing?language=english) |
| Sign Language product | $99.95 | [Sign Language pricing](https://www.rocketlanguages.com/pricing?language=sign-language) |

The Spanish page lists a six-month plan, a free trial and prices excluding applicable tax. Plan installments and any promotional checkout discount were not verified. Do not advertise an invented coupon, universal package price, course outcome or discount expiration.

The [consumer terms](https://www.rocketlanguages.com/terms) qualify lifetime access: it is a personal, nontransferable license; online access may be removed, with members entitled to download available resources before that happens. Content reuse rights are restricted. Do not equate “lifetime” with a guarantee that every interactive feature will remain hosted indefinitely.

The [refund policy](https://www.rocketlanguages.com/refund-policy) provides a 60-day period. For payment plans, it starts with the first payment and covers payments made within that period; after that, cancellation prevents further charges but payments are not refundable. iOS purchases are governed by Apple's discretion and a separately described period. Clarify purchase channel and timing rather than promising an identical refund across every route.

### Article opportunity and competition

Live searches used: `Rocket Languages review`, `Rocket Languages official affiliate ClickBank pricing refund`, and official refund/terms searches. Results included [The Mezzofanti Guild](https://www.mezzoguild.com/rocket-languages-review/), [FluentU](https://www.fluentu.com/blog/reviews/rocket-languages/), [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/via/rocket-languages-review/) and [Miami Herald](https://www.miamiherald.com/careers-education/rocket-languages-review/). This is a qualitative observation of specialized and major-publisher competition, not endorsement of those reviews. No search-volume or ranking-difficulty score was invented.

My assessment: strong purchase intent is plausible for branded course/price queries, but a new general buying journal needs a distinct audience and direct experience. One generic “best language app” list would be a weak expansion.

Potential original angles after approval and trial access:

- “Rocket Spanish Level 1 or the full bundle: a beginner's buying decision.” Use a documented sample-lesson evaluation and goals, not a fluency guarantee.
- “Buying a language course before a trip: trial checklist for US travelers.” Compare time, listening preferences, device needs and local conversation practice.
- “Lifetime language courses: what access and refunds actually mean.” Compare exact current contracts, not headline labels.

Include free/library options fairly. For example, [Gaston County Public Library's own guide](https://gastonlibrary.libguides.com/homework-topics/foreign-language-learning) lists Rocket access with a library card. Availability is library-specific, not nationwide; readers should check their own library before purchasing.

Recommendation: hold promotion unless the publisher can demonstrate the required education-sales record and is admitted. A later learning/travel content cluster would also need a real review documenting the selected course, level, date and testing method. For the existing home/kitchen focus, this is a lower editorial priority despite stronger marketplace economics.
