# New ClickBank offers for ShoppeCove

Research date: September 26, 2026. Audience: US readers arriving through organic Google search. This is an offer-selection report, not a revenue forecast or a hands-on product review.

## Recommendation

**Select Pianoforall as the strongest new commercial test, and 4 Foot Farm Blueprint as a smaller home/garden test.** Neither is a proven earner for this website. Pianoforall has stronger observed marketplace earnings and more inspectable product information; 4 Foot Farm is closer to the existing kitchen/home readership but pays much less per conversion.

Do not launch every niche at once. If keeping the publication focused on home and kitchen is the priority, start with the garden topic and treat piano as a deliberate expansion into home hobbies. If testing the stronger new offer is the priority, start with Pianoforall and develop a coherent learning-at-home section.

Matsato, Complete Plant-Based Cookbook, Brain Training for Dogs, Ultimate Small Shop, and existing Derila/decluttering work are excluded as new recommendations. No new articles, CMS records, registrations, purchases or seller messages were created during this research.

## What was checked

The live [ClickBank Marketplace](https://accounts.clickbank.com/marketplace.htm) was inspected in its public, logged-out browser interface. Seven candidates received a detailed review of seller pages, affiliate resources and available terms. Additional listings were screened for audience fit and current activity. Pianoforall, 4 Foot Farm and the physical Self-Sufficient Backyard checkout were inspected without buying or entering personal/payment information.

Public search results were used to observe competing content, not to invent US search volume, keyword difficulty or ranking forecasts. No Search Console, site conversion data or paid keyword dataset was available. Paid course contents were not accessed or tested. Seller statements about outcomes, historical sales and refund rates remain seller claims.

### 1. Pianoforall — the strongest new test

- **Observed purchase:** $49 complete digital download at ClickBank checkout with United States and USD selected. A blank ZIP meant state/local tax was not established. The checkout states a 60-day return/replacement period. [Official order page](https://pianoforall.com/order).
- **Affiliate route:** 60% advertised commission, vendor `piano4all`, and review-copy availability. No separate mandatory approval step was found. [Affiliate resources](https://pianoforall.com/affiliate-program).
- **Why selected:** comparatively stronger recent EPC and gravity, a concrete one-time package, and public sample material that can support an original evaluation.
- **Limitation:** piano is a new topic for the site. Branded searches already surface specialist piano-review sites, including [Pianist's Compass](https://pianistscompass.org/reviews/apps/pianoforall/) and [Piano Practice Lab](https://www.pianopracticelab.com/reviews/pianoforall-review/). Their presence shows competition, not a measured difficulty score; their product claims were not adopted as evidence.
- **First article:** “Pianoforall for adult beginners: cost, format and who it suits.” Explain the learning format, equipment needed, feedback limitations, access conditions and alternatives. Describe it as a research-based buying guide until someone documents actual use.
- **Original contribution:** a sample-lesson assessment, device/offline checklist and an explicit explanation of who would be better served by personal instruction. Avoid claims that students will play fluently within a set time.

Detailed evidence: [piano and sewing research](research/clickbank-expansion-hobbies-2026-09-26.md).

### 2. 4 Foot Farm Blueprint — relevant, but modest economics

- **Observed purchase:** $7 digital product at the US/USD ClickBank checkout, before any address-dependent tax. Its footer states 60 days for return/replacement. [ClickBank sales page](https://4footfarmblueprint.com/cb/).
- **Affiliate route:** 70% base commission advertised; an optional verified partner enrollment offers a higher rate. [Affiliate center](https://4footfarmblueprint.com/affiliate-center). No SEO prohibition was found in inspected material; this is not a comprehensive guarantee of all account permissions.
- **Why selected:** edible patio/container planning connects naturally to kitchen interests and buying decisions. The lower price could reduce buyer hesitation, but that is a hypothesis, not a measured advantage for this site.
- **Limitation:** $5.22 observed average commission makes large editorial spend hard to justify without substantial qualified traffic. Paid instructions, creator qualifications and harvest/savings claims were not independently validated. Free [University of Maryland Extension guidance](https://extension.umd.edu/resource/growing-vegetables-containers-and-salad-tables) is a meaningful alternative that readers should see.
- **First article:** “4 Foot Farm Blueprint: what the $7 guide includes and what to check first.” Add an original space/light/budget checklist and compare organized paid guidance with free Extension resources. Do not repeat the seller's food-independence or savings promises.
- **Attribution finding:** the Marketplace preview reaches `/cb/` and ClickBank checkout; the generic `/just4ft` page instead links to SamCart. Use the owner's genuine ClickBank HopLink and verify attribution later. A direct seller URL or Marketplace preview is not an earning link.

Detailed evidence: [garden research](research/clickbank-expansion-garden-2026-09-26.md).

### Why the higher-paying candidates are on hold

**Rocket Languages:** the best EPC among the seven does not make it currently accessible. The [Marketplace-linked registration route](https://affiliate.rocketlanguages.com/register) displayed a notice limiting new admission to affiliates with demonstrated education-technology sales success. Other publicly retrieved pages showed different application/rate information. Prioritize the actual admission notice and obtain account approval before treating it as available. A later article could compare Rocket Spanish Level 1 with its full bundle after a documented trial.

**VINCheckup:** attractive purchase intent and observed conversion activity, but its [ClickBank affiliate terms](https://www.vincheckup.com/affiliateNew/terms.php) state 65% while listing copy says 75%. Refund wording and the exact domain's NMVTIS data-supplier relationship also need clarification. Its absence from the inspected [DOJ approved-provider list](https://vehiclehistory.bja.ojp.gov/nmvtis_vehiclehistory) does not establish illegitimacy; it prevents an unsupported “approved provider” claim. A future article should distinguish VIN decoding from history reports and actual vehicle inspection. See [service-offer research](research/clickbank-expansion-services-2026-09-26.md).

**Self-Sufficient Backyard:** the physical book checkout showed $37 plus $9.99 shipping, before address-dependent tax. Its [affiliate page](https://www.selfsufficientbackyard.com/affiliates/) restricts Google while separately allowing affiliates' websites. This is ambiguous for Google organic traffic. Written clarification of that channel and honest discussion of limitations is needed. It otherwise has a plausible home/garden fit.

**Sewing Pattern Secrets:** its [affiliate page](https://sewingpatternsecrets.com/store-front1682693778531) requires whitelisting and prohibits the brand in article URL paths. Sales-page refund language conflicts with qualifications in [consumer terms](https://sewingpatternsecrets.com/terms-of-use). Higher commission does not resolve either issue.

**TedsWoodworking:** unresolved questions about current plan provenance, organization and the advertised count make it a poor editorial choice now. A [named plan designer's critical account](https://matt.wandel.ca/ted/index.html) concerns an earlier package; it is a due-diligence lead, not our finding about today's files. We have not inspected the current collection. See [home-offer research](research/clickbank-expansion-home-2026-09-26.md).

## Turning selection into useful organic content

Start with one buying decision per article, use a unique search intent and URL, and link to existing content only where the reader connection is useful. A list of loosely related high-commission products is not a coherent content strategy.

Each commercial article should clearly show what the product costs, what is included, its limitations, suitable and unsuitable buyers, alternative ways to solve the problem, sources, and when facts were checked. Add an affiliate disclosure near the recommendation. Place a descriptive “Check current price and course details” link after the relevant decision information. Avoid fake countdowns, invented discounts, unsupported ratings and claims of personal testing.

Google's [reviews guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/reviews-system) favors substantive original analysis; its [spam policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) identifies copied merchant descriptions without added value as thin affiliation. Original checklists, documented samples and fair comparisons are the content opportunity. Article length or keyword repetition alone does not establish that value.

The owner subsequently supplied the Pianoforall and 4 Foot Farm HopLinks. Both articles were created and published with those links on September 26, 2026; see [publication status and verification](clickbank-content-plan-2026-09-26.md#publication-status). The research-phase observations above remain a record of the selection decision.

Measure Google impressions/clicks by page, qualified outbound clicks, attributed sales, reversals and actual net commissions. Keep the traffic-source attribution consistent. Expand a topic after this site's data supports it; Marketplace EPC is neither revenue per blog visitor nor a safe paid-ad bid.
